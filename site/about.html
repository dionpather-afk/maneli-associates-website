Professional profile

Nontando Mzayifani

Nontando is publicly described in professional profiles as a financial accountant and tax practitioner with more than 12 years of accounting experience. Her background includes bookkeeping, payroll, financial reporting, tax work and the preparation of financial statements.

Her published education includes a Bachelor of Commerce in Financial Accounting from Damelin. Her LinkedIn profile lists professional qualifications including B Com Acc, BPA (SA) and GTP (SA), and associations with SAIBA and SAIT.

A public professional profile lists her as Financial Accountant / Director at Maneli Associates from 2016 to 2023. Her current role with the practice has not yet been confirmed.

OUR STORY

Established in 2014.
Focused on what matters.

Maneli Associates is an accounting and consulting firm established in 2014 under the management of Nontando Mzayifani. The firm is headquartered in Johannesburg, South Africa, and serves close corporations, private companies, non-profit organisations and individuals.

The public profile describes a team that includes registered accountants, senior audit consultants and junior staff, with access to IT consultants experienced in accounting software.
